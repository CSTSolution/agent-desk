Agent Desk

AI work, paid only when it passes inspection

Balance –
  1. Plan. Nemotron turns your request into a pipeline of AI services.
  2. Hold. You approve the quote in PayPal. It's an authorization: held, not charged.
  3. Run & inspect. Every output is checked: speech is transcribed back, translations are back-translated.
  4. Settle. PayPal captures only the steps that passed. The rest of the hold is released.
